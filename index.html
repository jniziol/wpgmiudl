FAQ

Who runs this league?

MODS runs the actual league — the schedule, the field time start and end dates. MIUDL registers team(s) into the league and organizes a fair draft so rosters are built by captains rather than assigned at random.

Why did the format change?

MODS moved from individual first-come-first-serve signup with MODS-assigned teams to team-based registration, where captains register a team and are free to create their own roster.

How many teams will there be?

8 is the maximum allowed in the league, but how many we actually field depends mainly on player interest — the more players who sign up, the more teams (and captains) we can support. The more captains we get registered on August 27 also helps secure spots, but player interest (looking for create teams of 10 players) is what really drives the number.

How does the draft work?

Captains register teams, players register their interest, and then we run a draft that builds balanced rosters from the pool of interested players.

How much will registration cost?

We anticipate the cost will be around $150 per player (payable to the captains), based on the team registration fee.

Do I have to be a MODS member to participate?

Yes. All players must have a valid MODS membership for the 2026/2027 seasons in order to be eligible for the draft.

When will the draft take place?

The draft will take place shortly after the teams registrations. All players who have indicated their interest will be emailed with additional details as the actual draft date nears.

How do I register or withdraw interest?

Fill out the player interest form linked above. If your plans change, email us before the draft and we'll take your name out of the pool.

Questions?

Ask about the draft, captaining a team, or anything else. We're happy to hear from you.
